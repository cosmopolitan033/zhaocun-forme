FORME is a personal creative development experiment by zhaocun.

Version 2

A digital sculpture garden with three procedural forms, chrome/copper/porcelain surfaces, reflective studio lighting, a museum setting and direct drag rotation. This version uses original procedural scene construction built with Three.js. No external 3D models or photographs are rendered.

Project source · Three.js — MIT
